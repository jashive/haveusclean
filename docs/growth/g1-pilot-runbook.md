# Growth Layer 1.0 — Controlled Pilot Runbook

## Purpose
This runbook defines the first controlled 20–30 prospect acceptance pilot for Have Us Clean Growth Layer 1.0. The pilot validates Growth intake, normalization, duplicate resolution, enrichment provenance, deterministic scoring, contact review, and human review without activating outreach or ServiceOS handoff.

## Hard Safety Gates
- Acceptance environment only.
- Never run against Production ServiceOS.
- `growth_layer_enabled` may be ON only in acceptance.
- `growth_outreach_enabled` must remain OFF.
- `growth_auto_followup_enabled` must remain OFF.
- `growth_serviceos_handoff_enabled` must remain OFF.
- Wave 6 remains out of scope.
- Pilot fixture records are synthetic and use `.example.invalid` domains.
- No email, SMS, call, LinkedIn, or other outbound action may be triggered from this pilot.
- Do not create or repair canonical ServiceOS business units or jurisdictions from the Growth workstream.

## Required Canonical Scopes Before Execution
The pilot must not be loaded until ServiceOS governance provides and verifies both acceptance scopes:

### Ontario
- Country: CA
- Subdivision: ON
- Currency: CAD
- Active canonical organization
- Active canonical Ontario business unit
- Active canonical Ontario jurisdiction

### Arizona
- Country: US
- Subdivision: AZ
- Currency: USD
- Active canonical organization
- Active canonical Arizona business unit
- Active canonical Arizona jurisdiction

The Growth adapter receives these IDs through runtime configuration. It never manufactures them.

## Pilot Fixture
Source: `tests/fixtures/growthG1PilotFixture.js`

Composition:
- 24 total synthetic prospects
- 12 Ontario
- 12 Arizona
- Cities: Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa
- Segments: property management, office, corporate/general office, medical, dental, industrial-office, vacation rental, multi-property operator
- Source lanes: Google Maps, office directories, medical directories, industrial parks, property-manager lists

Designed test cases include:
- one exact Ontario duplicate pair
- shared-domain/probable duplicate cases in Ontario and Arizona
- high-ICP prospects with zero buying-intent points until verified human/external intent evidence exists
- separate Ontario and Arizona canonical scope routing

## Execution Sequence
1. Verify the current Growth branch and acceptance project before any write.
2. Verify all four Growth feature gates; only core Growth may be ON.
3. Verify both governed ON and AZ canonical scopes.
4. Normalize all 24 fixture records using `normalizeLegacyLead` with the governed scope map.
5. Insert prospects through the controlled `growth-g1` boundary or service-role RPC.
6. Run deterministic duplicate resolution within organization + business unit + jurisdiction only.
7. Persist duplicate-review results.
8. Add sourced enrichment evidence. Every applied field must reference its evidence record.
9. Attempt at least one prohibited inferred identity-field update and verify rejection.
10. Add contact candidates and verify normalized email/phone/LinkedIn identity handling.
11. Apply deterministic `g1-rules-v1` scoring. Confirm AI alone cannot create intent points.
12. Human-review duplicate classifications, contacts, and field resolutions.
13. Complete review for eligible unique prospects. Confirm final status is `review_ready` only.
14. Verify exact duplicates are suppressed and cannot complete review.
15. Verify no canonical ServiceOS Revenue objects were created.
16. Verify outbound, auto-follow-up, and handoff gates remain OFF.
17. Verify audit events exist for creation, enrichment, scoring, contact review, duplicate review, field resolution, and review completion.
18. Roll back/remove pilot data unless governance explicitly approves retention of acceptance fixtures.

## Acceptance Criteria
PASS requires all of the following:
- 24/24 fixtures normalize successfully with correct jurisdiction separation.
- Ontario records resolve to CA/ON/CAD canonical scope.
- Arizona records resolve to US/AZ/USD canonical scope.
- Cross-scope duplicate matching is impossible.
- Intent remains zero without verified intent evidence.
- Exact duplicate fixture is suppressed.
- Shared-domain fixture does not auto-suppress as exact duplicate.
- Inferred evidence cannot overwrite identity fields.
- Human-accepted factual evidence can update allowlisted fields.
- Review completion rejects incomplete review state.
- Completed unique prospects stop at `review_ready`.
- No prospect becomes outreach eligible.
- No email/SMS/call/LinkedIn send occurs.
- No ServiceOS service_request, opportunity, estimate, quote, conversion, job, invoice, or payment record is generated by the pilot.
- An immutable Growth audit trail is present.

## Stop Conditions
Immediately stop the pilot if:
- either ON or AZ canonical scope is missing or mismatched;
- any Growth outbound/handoff gate is ON unexpectedly;
- any record appears in the wrong jurisdiction/business unit;
- any inferred value writes an identity field;
- any duplicate is matched across jurisdiction/business-unit boundaries;
- any ServiceOS Revenue record is created before governed handoff;
- any real-world outbound action is attempted.
